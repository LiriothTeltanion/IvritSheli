# A2 starter lexicon editorial notes

This file records narrow linguistic decisions for `starter_lexicon_v4.py`.
It is not a claim that every example has been externally certified.

## Editorial policy

- Store verbs as infinitives; do not infer root, binyan, or conjugation tables.
- Keep masculine and feminine first-person phrases separate when pronunciation
  differs but unpointed spelling can hide that difference.
- Prefer short, contemporary Israeli service situations.
- Use full niqqud as the reviewed form. Add `reading_hints` only where the
  unpointed spelling or meaning is particularly likely to confuse a learner.

## Known limits

- Romanization is a practical learner aid, not an ISO transliteration system.
- Gender labels describe the exact starter sense; they are not complete
  morphology.
- Examples deliberately avoid presenting a complete verb paradigm.
